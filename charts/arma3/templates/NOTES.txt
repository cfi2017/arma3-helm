Arma 3 is bootstrapping. Initial Steam downloads may take an hour or more.
  kubectl logs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} deployment/{{ include "arma3.fullname" . }} -c bootstrap -f
  kubectl logs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} deployment/{{ include "arma3.fullname" . }} -c arma3 -f

Keep Steam Guard enabled. If bootstrap waits for approval, use its logged
pod-specific command, or run:
  kubectl exec -it -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} <pod-name> -c bootstrap -- python3 /chart/runtime.py auth
Codes are hidden. Ctrl-C/D detaches; login continues waiting for up to
{{ .Values.steam.authTimeoutSeconds }} seconds. Steam authentication is retained on the Steam PVC.

#login <admin password>, take the Commander slot, then configure the campaign.
